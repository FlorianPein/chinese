# Learning Plan: Mandarin through Pinyin

**Learner:** total beginner; explanations in English, pronunciation compared to English *and* German
**Goal:** talking to people: introducing yourself, small talk, friends, hobbies, plans, feelings
**Script:** Pinyin only for now (no characters)
**Time:** one lesson is 10–15 min; skip days, or do several lessons at once

### Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Lesson style | Small steps, like Duolingo: each lesson brings only a little new content and practises it many ways, mixed with things from earlier lessons |
| Where lessons happen | In the web app ([Learn tab](https://florianpein.github.io/chinese/)). Each lesson also has a generated summary in `lessons/` for reading on GitHub |
| Everything pre-built | Lessons, exercises and all audio are stored in the repo. Nothing is generated when you start a lesson |
| Pinyin notation | Tone marks (`nǐ hǎo`) are the main form. Tone numbers are shown small underneath (<sub>ni3 hao3</sub>), switchable in settings. Neutral tone = `5` |
| Your answers | Either form is accepted everywhere (`nǐ hǎo`, `ni3 hao3`, `ni3hao3`) |
| Sound comparisons | English *and* German |
| Characters | Stored in the data, hidden everywhere for now |
| Personal details | None. Self-introduction lessons use example people plus "swap in your own" word lists |

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Little new, lots of practice.** A lesson introduces one sound or idea and 3–5 new words, then uses them in 30+ short exercises. The lesson stays 10–15 minutes long; it's the *new content* that's small.
2. **Every lesson repeats.** Each lesson starts with a warm-up on earlier lessons and mixes old words into its exercises. Mistakes come back at the end of the lesson until you get them right.
3. **Hear everything.** Every word, phrase and syllable has audio. Every sound is played in all its tones and in several example words.
4. **Tones from day one.** A wrong tone can change the word (`mǎi` buy vs `mài` sell), so tones are never optional.
5. **Phrases from real words.** Sentences are built only from words you've already learned. Sound lessons use real, useful words, not nonsense syllables.
6. **Speak out loud.** Every lesson ends with sentences to say aloud, and you can record yourself and compare with the original.
7. **Review beats new material.** After a lesson, its words go into spaced-repetition flashcards. If you've been away, review first.

**A01 in detail** (L01 and L02 are ready):

| Lesson | New sound | New words | Example sentences |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| A01-L01 ✅ | 1st tone (high) and 4th tone (falling) | `tā`, `hē`, `shì`, `bù`, `duì` | *Tā bù hē.* · *Duì, shì tā!* |
| A01-L02 ✅ | 3rd tone (low) | `wǒ`, `nǐ`, `hǎo` | *Wǒ bù hē.* · *Hǎo, wǒ hē.* · *Shì wǒ.* |

| A01-L05 | All five mixed, minimal pairs | `mǎi` / `mài`, `wèn` / `wěn` | *Māma mà mǎ ma?* |

Several Pinyin sounds are closer to German than to English, and the lessons use whichever is the better match:

- `ü` = German *ü* (`nǚ` ≈ *nü*), which English doesn't have
- `z` = German *z* in *Zeit* (*ts*), not English *z*
- `h` = rough like German *ch* in *Bach*, not soft English *h*
- `x` ≈ German *ch* in *ich*, but with the tongue pressed against the lower teeth
- `ou` ≈ English *go*: here English is the better match

## 3. What a lesson looks like

A lesson is a sequence of about 45 short steps (30+ of them exercises), about 10–15 minutes:

1. **Warm-up** (5–8 exercises): things from the last lessons.
2. **The new sound**: a short explanation with English and German comparisons, a tone diagram, and the sound played in all its tones and in several example words. Then quick listening drills.
3. **New words, one at a time**: each word with its audio, its meaning and its syllable in all four tones, followed straight away by 1–3 exercises on it.
4. **Short phrases**, built only from words you know, each with a word-by-word breakdown you can tap to hear.
5. **Mixed practice**: new and old words together.
6. **Say it aloud**: listen, repeat, and optionally record yourself and play it back next to the original.

Wrong answers come back at the end of the lesson. When you finish, its words and phrases join your flashcard reviews, first due the next day.

**Exercise types**

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| `intro` | read a short explanation, tap sounds to hear them |
| `new` | meet a word or phrase: audio, meaning, all tones / word-by-word |
| `hear` | listen, pick the Pinyin you heard (often differing only in tone) |
| `tone` | listen, pick the tone |
| `meaning` | hear or read a word, pick its meaning |
| `pinyin` | see the meaning, pick the right Pinyin |
| `match` | match Pinyin and meanings (tapping a Pinyin plays it) |
| `build` | build a sentence from word tiles |
| `type` | type the Pinyin for a meaning, or of what you hear |
| `speak` | say it aloud; record and compare |

**Audio.** Every word, phrase and sound has an MP3, generated offline by a Mandarin voice. The voice sometimes gets a tone wrong, so the build measures the pitch of every take and checks it against the tone it should have. It keeps the best of several takes, and if none is right, it pitch-edits the syllable into the correct tone shape. For A01-L01 and L02 that's 51 audio files: 39 natural takes, 11 pitch-corrected, and 1 the check can't measure (*Shì wǒ.*, where the two syllables run together).

## 4. Tools in this repository

| Tool | What it is | How you use it |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Web app** (`docs/`, GitHub Pages) | **Learn**: the lessons. **Review**: spaced-repetition flashcards for words you've learned. **Tones**: a tone-hearing quiz. **Listen**: all audio of a lesson. Progress is saved in your browser (export/import in ⚙︎) | Open it daily, on laptop or phone |
| **Lesson data** (`data/<lesson>.json`) | Each lesson's words, phrases (with hidden characters) and its exact sequence of steps | The source for everything else |
| **Lesson notes** (`lessons/<lesson>.md`) | A readable summary of each lesson: explanations, words, phrases, sounds, what it repeats | Generated from the data. Read on GitHub, or use for chat practice |
| **Audio** (`docs/audio/`) | One MP3 per word, phrase and sound, tone-checked; `manifest.json` records how each was made | Played by the app; 🐢 plays slowly |
| **Build tool** (`tools/build.py`) | Checks the lessons (e.g. no word is tested before it's taught, every answer is among the options), makes audio, writes the app data and lesson notes | Run after changing lesson data |
| **Progress log** (`PROGRESS.md`) | What you've done, and which sounds and words you struggle with | Updated after chat sessions; used to plan the next lessons |
| **Chat sessions** | *"Quiz me on A01"*, *"role-play meeting someone"* | Claude quizzes, corrects and adapts the next lessons |

`archive/` holds the first versions of A01 and U01-L01 (before lessons were split up). Their phrases and notes get reused for the new U01 lessons.

### Layout

```
PLAN.md  README.md  PROGRESS.md

  course.json        ← topics and lesson order
  A01-L01.json       ← one file per lesson: items + steps

  A01-L01.md         ← generated notes

  cards.json         ← generated from data/

  build.py  pinyin.py  requirements.txt

```

## 5. Your routine (flexible)

| Day type | What to do |
|----------|------------|
| **Normal day (10–15 min)** | Review if anything is due, then 1 lesson |
| **Busy day (2–5 min)** | Review only, which still counts |
| **Free day (30 min+)** | Review, 2–3 lessons, or a chat role-play |
| **Back after a break** | Review only until the due count is low, then continue |

Rule of thumb: **don't start a new lesson if more than ~40 flashcards are due.**

---

## 6. Milestones

- **After Track A + Unit 1:** read any Pinyin aloud with roughly the right sounds and tones.
- **After Unit 4:** hold a 1-minute self-introduction (name, where from, where you live, age, job).
- **After Unit 9:** have a short back-and-forth: ask about someone, talk hobbies, make a plan.
- **After Unit 13:** have a 5-minute simple conversation in Pinyin, with mistakes allowed.

Each milestone has a short "checkpoint" chat session.
